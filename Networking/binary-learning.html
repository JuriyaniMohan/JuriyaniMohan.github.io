Division Method

To convert any decimal number to binary: repeatedly divide by 2, collect remainders. Read them bottom to top.

Example: 172
172 ÷ 2 = 86 r 0
86 ÷ 2 = 43 r 0
43 ÷ 2 = 21 r 1
21 ÷ 2 = 10 r 1
10 ÷ 2 = 5 r 0
5 ÷ 2 = 2 r 1
2 ÷ 2 = 1 r 0
1 ÷ 2 = 0 r 1

128 + 32 + 8 + 4 = 172 ✓
